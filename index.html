About Me

Bridging classical price theory with mathematical game theory to evaluate market efficiency and institutional design.

Siwat Teimtad

Welcome to my research portal. As an economist, my analytical approach is anchored in two foundational paradigms: the Austrian/classical tradition of unintended consequences and decentralized knowledge, paired with the mathematical rigor of modern non-cooperative game theory.

My objective is to demystify complex macroeconomic phenomena and regulatory architectures by tracing how individual incentives aggregate into systemic equilibrium outcomes. True mastery of economics lies not in convoluted models, but in applying foundational truths consistently across all market conditions.
